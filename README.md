### About

I'm Max — online as **Legion112**. I care about how systems actually behave: processes, networks, disks, and the small tools that make a machine feel yours.

At home I build a homelab — networking, NAS plans, Docker on Linux, and Go utilities when the BIOS or the OS gets in the way. At work I'm a Tech Lead focused on backend and infrastructure. I spent years shipping PHP/Symfony; these days **Go** is what I enjoy writing most.

If you want the unfiltered version of that — experiments, ports, infra notes, and occasional rants — I publish on Telegram:

**→ [t.me/fearnotcode](https://t.me/fearnotcode)** (*Fearofcode*)
